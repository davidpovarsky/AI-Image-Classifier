# Recovery and uninstall

A protected uninstall authorization is single-use, short-lived, service-issued, and bound to the local uninstaller. The uninstaller disables capture, restores the exact proxy snapshot, verifies internet independence, stops engine/service, removes the exact CA fingerprint and product-owned firewall/IPC objects, then removes files.

Midway failure runs recovery before exit. Passwords are read through protected UI/IPC, never command-line arguments. A local administrator can still forcibly remove the product; no claim of impossible uninstall or stealth persistence is made.
